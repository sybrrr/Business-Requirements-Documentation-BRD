# 1. Executive Summary

**DIA** operates a network of behavioral health treatment centers (CTC clinics) serving patients in medication-assisted treatment programs. Clinicians currently lack a consolidated, real-time view of patient relapse risk across facilities, forcing them to rely on manual review of separate systems and clinical judgment alone.

The MyndWell Relapse Prediction Dashboard addresses this gap by analyzing relapse prediction models and risk level data to generate personalized intervention strategies for each patient. The platform surfaces risk levels (High, Medium, Low), prediction dates, current dosage information, and AI-driven recommendations at the point of care.

This BRD defines the business requirements for enhancing the MyndWell dashboard, including expanded search capabilities, improved filtering, EHR/EMR integration with the SMART system, HL7 data exchange, and direct user feedback mechanisms to continuously improve prediction accuracy.

## Business Problem

Clinicians do not have a fast, reliable way to identify which patients are at highest risk of relapse across facilities. Manual review is time-consuming, inconsistent, and delays intervention.

## Proposed Solution

Enhance the MyndWell dashboard to provide real-time, searchable, filterable relapse risk data integrated with existing clinical systems, with direct user feedback to improve AI predictions over time.

## Expected Outcomes

- Faster identification of high-risk patients
- Improved treatment planning and medication management
- Reduced readmission rates
- Increased clinician confidence in AI-driven recommendations
- Compliance with HIPAA, SOC 2, and ISO 27001 requirements
