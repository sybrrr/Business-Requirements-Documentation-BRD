# 16. Glossary

**AI (Artificial Intelligence):** Technology that enables systems to learn and make decisions.

**BRD (Business Requirements Document):** A document defining business needs and requirements.

**CAPA:** Corrective and Preventive Action.

**CTC:** Comprehensive Treatment Center.

**EHR (Electronic Health Record):** Digital version of a patient's medical history.

**EMR (Electronic Medical Record):** Digital record of a patient's medical treatment.

**FMEA:** Failure Mode and Effects Analysis.

**HIPAA:** Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act.

**HL7:** Health Level Seven — a standard for exchanging healthcare data.

**Methasoft:** A medication dispensing and patient management system.

**MuleSoft:** An integration platform for connecting systems and APIs.

**PHI (Protected Health Information):** Health information that identifies an individual.

**QMS:** Quality Management System.

**SMART:** A clinical software system used for patient records.

**SOC 2:** Service Organization Control 2 — a compliance framework.

**UAT (User Acceptance Testing):** Testing performed by end users to validate requirements.
