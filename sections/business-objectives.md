# 2. Business Objectives

| # | Objective | Success Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reduce time to identify high-risk patients | Time from login to risk view | Under 30 seconds |
| 2 | Improve clinician adoption | Weekly active users | 80% of clinical staff |
| 3 | Improve prediction accuracy | User feedback thumbs-up rate | 85% positive |
| 4 | Reduce patient readmissions | 30-day readmission rate | 15% reduction |
| 5 | Achieve compliance readiness | Audit findings | Zero critical findings |
| 6 | Improve treatment planning | Clinician-reported confidence | 90% report improved confidence |
